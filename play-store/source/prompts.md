# Image-generation prompts

The two PNG masters in this directory were created with the built-in image-generation workflow using the existing Glow Words `og.png` and `letter-garden.webp` as visual references.

## App icon master

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Google Play app icon and Android launcher icon source
Input images: Image 1 and Image 2 are visual references for the established Glow Words firefly character, magical night garden, lantern glow, tactile letter stones, and color palette.
Primary request: Create a polished square icon for the Glow Words children's word-search game, faithfully matching the reference art direction. Feature one cheerful golden firefly as the unmistakable focal subject, facing the viewer and holding a tiny glowing lantern, with a subtle path of colorful letter stones behind it.
Style/medium: premium friendly 3D storybook illustration, soft tactile materials, crisp readable silhouette at small sizes.
Composition/framing: square, full-bleed deep violet night-garden background; firefly centered and large, face and warm lantern glow readable at 48px; generous visual safe area for dynamic icon masking.
Lighting/mood: magical, warm, curious, welcoming.
Color palette: deep indigo and violet background, golden yellow firefly and lantern, small coral, mint, sky-blue and purple accents.
Constraints: no words, no letters used as a logo, no typography, no watermark, no badge, no border, no rounded corners, no external drop shadow; keep essential face and lantern inside the central 66% safe zone; output a single square icon artwork.
```

## Feature graphic master

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Google Play feature graphic master, designed for a final 1024 x 500 crop
Input images: Image 1 is the primary reference for the established firefly, lantern, magical garden, tactile letter stones, lighting, and palette. Image 2 is a supporting brand reference only.
Primary request: Create a wide cinematic banner that immediately communicates the Glow Words children's word-search game. Show the cheerful golden firefly carrying its glowing lantern above a magical garden path made of colorful tactile letter stones. The stones should suggest a word-search adventure, with a few clear individual alphabet letters but no phrase.
Style/medium: premium friendly 3D storybook illustration matching the references closely.
Composition/framing: very wide landscape composition; firefly and lantern grouped near the horizontal center; letter-stone path leads inward from the lower foreground; simple deep-violet starry garden background; important subject matter remains inside the central 70% so the image survives responsive cropping.
Lighting/mood: warm lantern glow, curious, magical, welcoming.
Color palette: deep indigo and violet with golden yellow, coral, mint, sky-blue, and soft pink accents.
Constraints: no app icon, no title, no tagline, no typography, no logo, no watermark, no device frame, no border; avoid tiny clutter at the edges; single continuous scene.
```
