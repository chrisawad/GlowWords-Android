## App name

Glow Words

## Short description

Race the clock to find hidden words and make the whole board glow.

## Full description

Turn every word search into a little adventure.

Glow Words is a colorful, touch-friendly word search game for growing readers and puzzle fans. Choose an age range, tune the timer, word count, and grid size, then swipe across the board to uncover every hidden word.

Follow the glowing word trail:

- Pick an age-matched challenge for readers ages 5 and up
- Choose grids from 8 × 8 through 16 × 16
- Adjust the timer and number of hidden words
- Drag across letters in any straight line
- Hear each discovered word spoken and spelled aloud
- Practice saying words with the optional microphone activity
- Keep playing with built-in word lists when the online word source is unavailable

Every round mixes a bright letter board, friendly feedback, and a little bit of magic. Find every word before time runs out and make the whole board glow.

Glow Words contains no advertising and does not require an account.
