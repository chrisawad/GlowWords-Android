# Glow Words Privacy Policy — draft

**Effective date:** [DATE]

Glow Words is a word-search game that does not require an account and does not contain advertising.

## Information used by the game

Glow Words connects to its first-party web service over HTTPS to load the game and request age-appropriate word lists. If the word service is unavailable, the game uses word lists bundled with the application. Like most hosted services, the server provider may process standard connection information such as IP address, request time, device or browser details, and requested URL for security and operational purposes. The game may store first-party cookies or local browser storage on the device to support its web experience. Glow Words does not require a user profile or account.

## Optional microphone activity

Players can choose a pronunciation-practice activity. Glow Words asks for microphone permission only when the player starts that activity. The device's speech-recognition service converts the spoken word into text so the game can compare it with the practice word and provide feedback. Glow Words does not intentionally store the microphone recording. The device or speech-recognition provider may process audio according to that provider's terms and privacy practices. Players can deny microphone access and continue using the word-search game and listen-only practice features.

## Advertising and accounts

Glow Words does not display advertising, sell personal information, or require users to create an account.

## Children's privacy

Glow Words is designed for growing readers, including children. We aim to collect only the information needed to operate the game and its optional practice feature. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions or requests using the address below.
